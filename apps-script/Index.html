跟新朋友打招呼Say hello to a new friend

到訪 APSN Tanglin School，練習自我介紹，聽聽當地學生怎樣分享自己的生活。At APSN Tanglin School, practise introducing yourself and listen to local students talk about their lives.

很高興認識你！“Nice to meet you!”
↗

用英語點一餐Order a meal in English

在熟食中心看餐牌、試着點餐和問價。知道怎樣開口，下次便可以再試。Read a menu at a hawker centre, try ordering and ask for the price. A little practice makes it easier to try again.

我想要這個，謝謝。“I would like this, please.”
↗

從街道認識文化Discover culture on the street

走過小印度、牛車水和甘榜格南，觀察街道上的語言、食物和建築，和香港的日常生活作比較。In Little India, Chinatown and Kampong Gelam, notice the languages, food and buildings. Compare what you see with everyday life in Hong Kong.

有哪些相似的地方？又有哪些不同？“What is similar? What is different?”
